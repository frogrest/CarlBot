# Reference Screenshot Inventory

Source: [`reference/screenshots/reference.pdf`](../../reference/screenshots/reference.pdf), six pages. The reference is a compact desktop helpdesk: white/light-gray surfaces, small dense text, pale-blue tables, blue linked subjects, and restrained orange/red priority accents. The replica uses the same information hierarchy and density with fictional CarlBot branding and synthetic records.

## Page 1 — Open ticket queue

- **Layout:** centered application canvas; brand/header strip at top, two compact horizontal navigation rows, then search/sort controls and a full-width queue table.
- **Navigation:** primary modules (`Dashboard`, `Users`, `Tasks`, `Tickets`, `Knowledgebase`); ticket actions (`Open`, `My Tickets`, `Closed`, `Search`, `New Ticket`).
- **Typography:** small sans-serif UI text; compact bold column labels and blue ticket/subject links.
- **Spacing and surfaces:** tight rows, thin gray grid lines, alternating white and pale-blue rows, almost no shadows or rounded-card styling.
- **Queue columns:** ticket ID, last updated, location, subject, sender, priority, assignee. Row checkboxes, sort affordances, toolbar icons, and a visible search field.
- **Status/color:** green section indicator; muted blue-gray table; urgent priority shown with a warm red/pink fill.
- **Replica use:** queue view, navigation, search/filter bar, priority/status badges, ticket rows.

## Page 2 — Ticket detail and conversation

- **Layout:** same header and ticket navigation; compact ticket action toolbar; title and subject; two-column metadata blocks; location; tabs; vertically stacked ticket-thread entries.
- **Hierarchy:** ticket ID first, then subject, status/priority/department, owner and assignment details, followed by the conversation.
- **Conversation:** light-blue message header, bordered white body, small avatar blocks, author/time attribution, replies and inline evidence/attachments.
- **Replica use:** detail header, ticket metadata, activity/conversation thread, diagnostics and evidence alongside the thread.

## Page 3 — Ticket reply composer

- **Layout:** continuation of the detail thread. Recent activity/history appears above a tabbed `Post Reply` / `Post Internal Note` composer and a simple footer.
- **Controls:** sender, recipients, reply-to, canned-response selector, compact formatting toolbar, large text area, ticket-status selector, and submit/reset buttons.
- **Surfaces:** pale blue-gray tabs and borders; primary submit button uses a restrained yellow/orange highlight.
- **Replica use:** ticket notes and reply composer; distinguish technician-authored notes from AI-generated investigation notes.

## Page 4 — New ticket form

- **Layout:** same application chrome above a single-column, form-heavy content region.
- **Form pattern:** gray section bars separate collaborators, ticket information, response, and internal note groups. Labels align in a narrow left column; native-looking inputs/selects fill the right.
- **Controls:** user/contact selection, notification, source, help topic, department, SLA, due date, assignee, response editor, ticket status and signature.
- **Replica use:** compact filter controls, lab fault/asset forms and note entry; preserve labeled form rows and section headings.

## Page 5 — Canned response content

- **Layout:** continuation of the new-ticket editor, focused on the response body.
- **Editor:** canned-response selector and append toggle above a minimal rich-text toolbar and large bordered text area.
- **Content:** short paragraphs, numbered follow-up questions, and a synthetic equipment photo embedded in the response.
- **Replica use:** safe, evidence-backed suggested response and compact response composer. Do not reuse the photo or customer wording as live data.

## Page 6 — Internal note and submit controls

- **Layout:** bottom of the same long form; attachment area, ticket status and signature controls, internal-note editor, then `Open`, `Reset`, and `Cancel` buttons.
- **Editor details:** compact formatting toolbar, large empty text region, clear gray section header.
- **Replica use:** internal technician note workflow and explicit submit/cancel states. No destructive action is associated with chat clearing.

## Cross-page visual rules

- Keep the primary workflow dense and information-led rather than converting it into a generic dashboard.
- Use a modest top-level brand/nav band and compact secondary action row.
- Prefer aligned metadata, thin borders, compact tables, blue links, pale blue row alternation, and small semantic status/priority colors.
- Add modern polish only through consistency, legibility, restrained typography and careful interaction states; preserve the reference's service-desk character.
- The AI panel is an additive technician aid, not a replacement for the queue, thread, evidence, or ticket status.
- All names, ticket data, assets, images, locations, and timestamps in the replica are synthetic.
